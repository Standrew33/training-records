# Training records – short report

**What I built and why.** A C# / .NET 10 solution: `Skoleni.Core` (obligations, validity status, planner), `Skoleni.Data` (cleaning of the four exports), `Skoleni.DataPrep` (raw CSV → cleaned `dataset.json`) and `Skoleni.Web` (Blazor WebAssembly UI). It is a static site on GitHub Pages: free, no server or accounts, and no data leaves the browser. National ID numbers from the exports never reach it – cleaning happens beforehand and the models have no field for them. Screens: Overview (what needs attention, status by training and department), People (filters, CSV export), Sign-up plan (whom to put on which term), Terms and Data quality, which lists every data fix and what was done about it – nothing is corrected silently.

**What I found in the data, and my assumptions.** The exports are deliberately dirty (23 errors, 66 warnings, 111 minor items): Windows-1250 encoding, five date formats incl. Excel numbers and a 1900 placeholder, comment/total rows, repeated record IDs, wrong course codes, 4 duplicate people and 9 records for unknown IDs, inconsistent or missing departments, and in terms an impossible date, a duplicate, zero capacity, overbooked and weekend terms. Unclear codes (`BOZP-X`) are dropped and reported, not guessed. Assumptions: validity comes from the catalogue, not from `platnost_do` (differs for 34 records – I do not know which is right); `0 months` = never expires; parental leave and terminated staff are neither counted nor planned; new hires get 30 days; "expiring soon" = 90 days; "today" = the export date, 1 Oct 2026.

**Result and doubts.** The planner puts people past the deadline first, then by expiry, each on the nearest term with a free seat. It is covered by tests, including a comparison with brute force. It gives 201 sign-ups (about CZK 162k), but for 55 people validity ends before any term can take them (43 in cybersecurity) – a case for scheduling an earlier term. I did not compare other planning policies.

**Not done.** The app does not know who already holds the "booked" seats; sign-ups are not stored or sent; optional courses are not evaluated; no UI tests.

**Next.** Confirm assumptions with HR; a system of record for sign-ups with notifications to managers; uploading an export in the app; manual plan edits; fixing data errors at the source.
